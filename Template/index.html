CLEAR TRANSPARENCY

FREQUENTLY ASKED
INQUIRIES

We believe in straightforward, transparent communication about timelines, scopes, pricing, and deliverables from day one.

CONFIGURE A SPRINT
Our flagship sprint cycles typically span 6 to 12 weeks depending on scope. Brand identity sprints take 4–6 weeks, while bespoke flagship WebGL platforms and spatial web configurators operate on 8–10 week agile delivery cadences with continuous staging previews and bi-weekly milestone demonstrations.
We provide fully typed design tokens, zero-drift component repositories, Git worktrees, and documented WebGL / Three.js shader modules. We can either lead end-to-end full-stack implementation or integrate as an embedded senior creative engineering pod with your engineering leadership.
We partner with venture-backed startups, global cultural institutions, and Fortune 500 enterprises through two primary models: fixed-deliverable milestone sprints for discrete flagship platforms, and quarterly studio retainers for continuous product evolution and experimental R&D.
You retain 100% full, unencumbered ownership of all final designs, codebases, custom shader algorithms, 3D assets, vector libraries, and design system tokens upon final milestone sign-off.
Every digital artifact we ship is audited for strict performance budgets: zero bulky third-party runtime dependencies, hardware-accelerated CSS transforms, WebGL level-of-detail (LOD) degradation fallbacks for mobile GPUs, and consistent 95+ Google Lighthouse scores across all breakpoints.
